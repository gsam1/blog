## Getting Started

Posts live in the `_posts/` directory and must be named `YYYY-MM-DD-title.md`.

Each post needs this front matter at the top:

```yaml
---
layout: post
title: "Your Post Title"
date: YYYY-MM-DD
categories: your-category
---
```

Then write your content in Markdown below.
